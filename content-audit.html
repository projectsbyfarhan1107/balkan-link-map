Title audit

Every live page was read in full. For each one: what the current title promises, what the content actually delivers, and an accurate replacement title and meta description where they don't match. Click a card for the evidence, anchor phrases and URL.

Permalink changes

URLs that are clearly broken: spelling errors, too long, keyword-stuffed, or describing a different topic. URLs that are fine but not perfect are left alone. Every change below needs a 301 redirect from the old URL. The redirect list is drafted here and can be set up later.

Content problems

Pages where a new title alone can't fix the problem (too thin, off-topic, or broken), plus copy errors the reviewers spotted while reading, such as wrong borough names, wrong counts, garbled text and missing sections. No articles were rewritten. These are flagged for a writer.

Overlapping articles

Pairs of articles that cover nearly the same topic. They compete with each other in search. Consider merging them, or giving each a clearly different angle (the proposed titles already try to separate them where possible).
